# AFIT Digital Twin Lab — Cyber-Physical OODA Loop Testbed

* **Institution:** Air Force Institute of Technology (AFIT), Department of Operational Sciences (Wright-Patterson AFB, OH)
* **Associated Publication:** [Walton, R. B., & Ciarallo, F. W. (2022). "A Framework to Manage Systems Based on Digital Twin." *Proceedings of the IISE Annual Conference & Expo 2022*.](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=view_citation&hl=en&user=eeIWVBkAAAAJ&citation_for_view=eeIWVBkAAAAJ:u-x6o8ySG0sC)
* **Tech Stack:** Simio, Visual Basic (.NET), Python, Raspberry Pi, Spatial Tracking Sensors

## Overview
Developed as Technical Lead at the AFIT Digital Twin Lab to implement automated bidirectional communication between physical robotic/sensor hardware and discrete-event simulation models in **Simio**. Operationalizes the Digital Twin **OODA Loop (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act)** framework by ingesting real-time physical telemetry, simulating predictive alternatives in Simio via Visual Basic extensions, and actuating autonomous control decisions back onto physical hardware via Python.
